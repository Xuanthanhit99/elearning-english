# Product Scope Baseline — BeaconVie V1 Complete
## Product thesis
BeaconVie evolves from an English learning site to an AI-assisted multilingual learning platform for Vietnamese learners, with internationalization readiness. Differentiator: demonstrate the ability to use a language in real-world situations with inspectable evidence of progress, not just lesson counts.

## Platforms
- Public SEO website + authenticated learning web (Next.js).
- Installable PWA with truthful offline support and secure cache policies.
- Native Android/iOS app (Expo/React Native) with shared API, voice/microphone, notifications, offline/sync where supported.
- Admin/content QA, partner and affiliate dashboards with role-based access.

## Learning and AI
- Existing English foundation: placement, vocabulary, grammar, reading, listening, speaking, writing, CEFR pathway, missions, arena/community.
- Beacon AI Coach 2.0: consent-based learner memory, goal-aware tutoring, explain → practice → feedback → review → reassess, memory controls/deletion.
- Real-Life Mission Simulator: job interview, travel, workplace, restaurant, daily communication; record/replay/retry, context-sensitive rubrics, calibrated scoring.
- Adaptive Daily Mission: 10–15-minute personalized sessions based on evidence, spaced repetition, skill gaps and available time.
- Language DNA: per-language skill map, recurring mistakes, mastery/confidence and provenance.
- Pronunciation & Writing Repair Studio: targeted correction, before/after, evidence-based explanations, avoid invented precision.
- Proof of Progress: learner-controlled portfolio, mission history, assessment evidence; no unsupported official certification claims.
- Learn From Real Life: consented user-submitted text/photos/documents transformed into safe practice, moderation/copyright/privacy protections.
- Cooperative Language Missions: pair/group practice, moderation and abuse safeguards.
- Learning Passport: one identity, distinct enrollments, proficiency frameworks and progress per target language.

## Language roadmap *inside V1 architecture*
- English: full existing product plus quality gates.
- Mandarin Chinese: first new-language **foundation/beta**, including Pinyin, tones, Hanzi, early HSK-aligned content with subject-matter QA.
- Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish: catalog/framework/capability readiness; content releases ONLY when validated. No fake complete courses.
- Distinguish source/explanation language from target language; CEFR/HSK/JLPT/TOPIK not numerically interchangeable.

## Commercial ecosystem
- Free and Premium (hypothesis 99k–149k VND monthly; validate AI unit economics and demand).
- Checkout, payment state, server-side entitlements, subscriptions, receipts, refund and reconciliation.
- First-party referral/affiliate: attribution, qualified conversion, hold/reversal, ledger, payout approvals, fraud controls, affiliate dashboard.
- Curated educational resource store with licensing and editorial QA.
- Tutor/teacher verified profiles, partner directory and approved pilot.
- Academy/organization pilot: cohort assignments and privacy-scoped reports.
- Full tutor booking, split payouts, self-service marketplace: V1 architecture/controlled release gate; do not launch without dispute, payout, compliance and operational QA.

## Acquisition, brand, retention
- Technical SEO, indexable content hub, structured data, canonical, sitemap/robots, server-rendered public pages.
- Conversion-focused landing/guest try, placement, first lesson and return journey.
- Analytics funnel: landing_view → trial_start → placement_complete → signup_complete → lesson_complete; consent and privacy.
- Blue BeaconVie brand/logo; real teaching media and no unapproved redesign; consistent short-form educational content.
- Current baseline: user reported ~7 users (registration vs active status not yet verified); do not treat as measured analytics.

## Non-goals and safeguards
No ad-heavy learning interface, multilevel affiliate pyramid, fabricated AI scores, unsupported offline claims, unauthorized recordings, cross-language progress mixing, or fabricated certificates.
