# SEO, Brand, Activation & Commerce
## Starting observation
User reports approximately 7 users; need distinguish signups, MAU, activated and retained. Do not assert cause without Google Search Console and analytics.

## SEO priorities
Technical: robots/sitemap, canonical, SSR, metadata, indexability, internal links, performance and mobile.
Content candidates (keyword research pending): free placement, English from zero, A1 roadmap, present/past simple, daily vocabulary, conversation phrases, A1 listening transcript, speaking with AI, A1 exercises.
Each page must have substantial learner value, not AI-generated thin doorway content; CTA into free first lesson.

## Activation funnel
landing_view → trial_start → placement_complete → signup_complete → lesson_complete → day-7 return. Instrument privacy-safe source, cohort and dropoff. Validate by real-user interviews, not just ratios from tiny samples.

## Brand
Retain BeaconVie logo blue, Vietnamese-first UI for local audience, desktop sidebar and fixed mobile bottom navigation. Show Coach + Real-Life Missions + Proof of Progress as brand differentiators. Compare actual screenshots with locked masters at desktop 1536/mobile 390.

## Revenue design
- Free tier: placement, foundational lessons, capped AI.
- Premium: trial price hypothesis 99k–149k VND/month, contingent on willingness to pay and AI unit economics.
- First-party referral/affiliate: single-tier commission hypothesis 15% on qualified first settled Premium purchase; never guarantee income; fraud and refund windows.
- Resource store: curated licensed digital resources.
- Verified teacher/partner directory and B2B academy pilot; booking and split payouts only after safeguards.
- Avoid ad-heavy course UX and uncontrolled self-service marketplace.
- Payment provider selection must consider Vietnam and mobile store policy; no provider is approved by this document.

## Operational metrics
Acquisition: impressions, clicks, CTR, indexed URLs, organic signups.
Learning: first-lesson activation, session completion, D7 retention, real-world mission success.
Commercial: trial→paid, ARPPU, gross margin after AI and payment fees, refund rate, affiliate CAC, reconciliation mismatch.
Safety: AI feedback disagreement, user deletion compliance, tutor moderation, chargebacks.
